Senior UX designer

Growing AI Search into a platform

AI Search served one product and answered every question the same way, leaving people waiting on a blank screen. Other teams wanted it on their own documents too.

View case study (slides)
Role
Ran customer discovery and weekly feedback and retention reporting, designed streaming answers, citations, and conversation management, proposed search-effort controls, and shipped 22 PRs, from small updates to full features.
Outcome
Weekly queries grew from about 200 to 700 and mean latency fell from 95s to 45s, with a 16s time to first token. AI Search now covers multiple document types and structured data, and reaches outside AI apps through MCP.
